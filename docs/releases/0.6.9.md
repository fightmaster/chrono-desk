# Chrono Desk 0.6.9

Task: CHR-DESK-001. Docs-Impact: LOCAL.

Count the captures currently awaiting a participant and number them from 1 to N.
Deleting a capture from any position closes the numbering gap, including in
the capture drawer. Once the list is empty, the next capture is number 1 even
when its storage ID is higher. Binding a capture removes it from this pending
list and recalculates the remaining positions. Failed deletion keeps the row
and count visible.

Browser acceptance covers three rapid captures, failed deletion, middle-row
deletion, drawer numbering, clearing the list, adding again and reopening the
event. Existing capture IDs, timestamps and stored results are preserved.
